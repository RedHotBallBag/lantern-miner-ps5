# What's changed

## v0.9.2 beta (Build 16)

*Following complaints from the management, several things down the mine have been put right.*

- **Dying for profit has been outlawed.** Gems you've already pocketed stay pocketed, and keys picked up a second time are
  worth precisely nothing. Reloading won't help you either
- **The thing from the cursed chest** has been asked to slow down in the early caverns. It has agreed, with poor grace
- **The Phoenix Feather** now hands over all three lives. It had been keeping one back
- **Clockwork Slow** no longer slows the lifts. Nobody wanted to wait longer for a lift. Nobody ever has
- **Dangling ladders** no longer give you a free jump off the bottom. There is nothing there. There never was
- **The left stick** wants less of a shove, and has stopped flinging you up ladders you were only walking past
- **Delver's Cache** won't take your money when you're already stuffed with everything in it
- **Old Barnaby** now shows your lives and continues, and Up from the top shelf takes you to the way out
- **Personal bests** are remembered after Save & Quit, so you can be congratulated properly
- **The air bottle** glows blue, as an air bottle should
- **The top of the screen** is better behaved when you're carrying a lot of gear

Your save carries over. Your dignity may not.
